# Roadmap

## Near term

- broker-neutral CSV mapping profiles;
- calendar and session heatmaps;
- configurable R-multiple calculations from entry, stop, target, and exit;
- richer playbook review and checklist scoring;
- encrypted backup export without disabling at-rest encryption.

## Later

- chart annotation and local screenshot attachments;
- replay-focused manual backtesting sessions;
- Monte Carlo and risk-of-ruin analysis;
- optional end-to-end encrypted sync with a self-hosted relay;
- plugin adapters for broker exports without embedding broker credentials.

TradeVault will remain useful without an account, subscription, or remote service.
